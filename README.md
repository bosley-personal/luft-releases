# Luft

Luft is a native Mac app for studying chess. It brings your games, opening repertoire, engine analysis, and practice into one place, so a game you played can become a line to review or a puzzle to solve.

## What you can do

- **Keep a personal game library.** Import PGN files or fetch your games from lichess and chess.com. Organize them in databases and collections, then search, annotate, and export them.
- **Analyze with Stockfish.** Stockfish 19 is included for playing and analysis. Review evaluations, recommended moves, and threats on the board.
- **Build an opening repertoire.** Arrange main lines, sidelines, and candidate moves; compare your games with your repertoire to find where you went off course.
- **Practice what you study.** Review repertoire lines, find puzzles in your own games, train tactics and endgames, play against the engine, and try Guess the Move.
- **Explore reference games.** An optional database adds millions of games, opening move statistics, and position search. Luft can check for new *The Week in Chess* issues.

## Download and install

Luft runs on macOS 14 or later on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. Open the [latest release page](https://github.com/bosley-personal/luft-releases/releases/latest), download **Luft.dmg** under Assets, open it, and drag Luft.app to the Applications shortcut.

This release is not Apple notarized. If macOS blocks the first launch, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**. Luft checks for signed app updates automatically after installation.

On first launch, Luft offers the optional reference database download. It uses about 6 GB when installed; allow about 11 GB free during the download and installation. You can add it later from **Settings → Reference**.

The other release assets are for updates and licensing: **Luft.zip** is the automatic update archive, and the Stockfish archive contains its GPLv3 license and source code. The reference database includes [Lumbra's Gigabase](https://lumbrasgigabase.com/en/) data under CC BY-NC 4.0 for noncommercial use and games from [The Week in Chess](https://theweekinchess.com/).
